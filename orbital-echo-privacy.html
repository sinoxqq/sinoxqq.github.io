Orbital Echo: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Orbital Echo ("the game") is a mobile rhythm game published by sinoxqq. This policy explains what data the game handles.

Data the game stores

The game saves your progress (scores, unlocked items, settings) only on your device. It has no accounts and no login, and we do not run servers that collect your game data.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, app interaction and diagnostic data. This is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change that choice at any time under Settings → Privacy & ad choices in the game. You can also reset or opt out of your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play store page.
